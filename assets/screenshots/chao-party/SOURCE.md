The creator says GPT-6 Astra ported Chao Garden to a Three.js browser game with multiplayer. The original post directly links chao.party. This is the creator’s account, not an independently audited development history.

## Verification scope

Selected a character and entered a shared garden as a guest. Chao, another player and pick/pet prompts were visible. Long-term pet progression was not tested. Unofficial Sonic fan project; original characters and assets retain their owners’ rights. No login or payment was required for the checked path. Mobile compatibility was not tested.
