## Model attribution

The creator says an early-access Astra prompt recreated a racing demo with an Ethiopian setting. This is the creator’s account, not an independently audited development history.

## Verification scope

Started the standard Grand Prix with five AI rivals; race timer and standings advanced. The menu also offers time trial, difficulty and graphics settings. Keyboard driving, slide and boost are documented. A complete three-lap race was not tested. No login or payment was required for the checked path. Mobile compatibility was not tested.
