## Browser verification — 2026-09-15

Opened the public game in desktop Chrome without logging in, paying, downloading a game package or installing anything. Selecting **ENTER THE GARDEN** started **The Pale Undergrowth**, chamber 1 of 6 in the tested edition. The live game displayed health, silk, ember seeds and enemies. After keyboard input, the enemy count fell from four to three and two ember seeds were collected. The subsequent death screen recorded one creature defeated and two seeds retained; **DESCEND AGAIN** started a fresh run.

The displayed controls are A/D to move, W to jump, S to drop, Space to dash, arrow keys to strike, Q to fire a bolt and E to mend. The opening combat and restart flow were exercised. The menu advertises permanent health upgrades saved in the current browser, but those purchases, every individual move, later chambers, the final encounter and mobile/controller compatibility were not tested. This is an access and opening-gameplay check, not a complete playthrough.

The creator reports asking Astra for a Silksong-style game and refining gameplay with two additional prompts. This is a creator-reported development workflow, not an independent audit of model usage or a one-prompt claim. The catalog does not present Thornwake as an official Silksong release.
