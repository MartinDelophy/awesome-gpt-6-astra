## Model attribution

The creator describes building the FPS with GPT-6 Astra Ultra and Blender-rendered artists, following his Van Gogh Town experiment. This is the creator’s account, not an independently audited development history.

## Verification scope

Selected an artist and entered the match scene with health and ammunition HUD. Mouse capture was unavailable in the embedded browser; active combat and a complete match were not verified. Loading can take tens of seconds. No login or payment was required for the checked path. Mobile compatibility was not tested.
