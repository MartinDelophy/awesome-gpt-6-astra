## Review — 2026-09-26

The public URL returned the JellyBlob game page without authentication. Its metadata describes a free multiplayer jelly game involving droplets, jumping over trails and boosting. The creator reports development through three large GPT-6 Astra sessions; this is attribution to the creator, not an independent audit of development history.

The submission describes desktop/mobile WebGL support with keyboard/mouse or touch and no sign-in. No interactive browser was available during this review, so movement, multiplayer connectivity and mobile controls were not independently play-tested.
