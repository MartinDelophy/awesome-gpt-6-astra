# Zork · The Great Underground Empire

- Creator: [Ethan Mollick](https://x.com/emollick)
- Access: https://zork-underground-empire.netlify.app/
- Creator statement: https://x.com/emollick/status/2096047660662722620
- Checked: 2026-09-09, desktop browser.

## Verification scope and model attribution

Started the expedition and entered the West of House scene, with movement/interact controls, inventory counter and quest HUD. The creator describes Astra adapting the text adventure’s plot and puzzles into a 3D action adventure and constructing its environments and characters in Three.js. This is an unofficial adaptation. Puzzles, combat and a full expedition were not completed. Initial loading took several minutes.

Model attribution follows the creator’s public statement, not an independent audit. Mobile compatibility was not tested.
