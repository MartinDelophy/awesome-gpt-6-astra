- Creator: [BPCKeeeeeepOn](https://github.com/BPCKeeeeeepOn), as credited in their [submission #143](https://github.com/MartinDelophy/awesome-gpt-6-astra/issues/143).

## Access check — 2026-11-16 (Asia/Shanghai)

Opened the public title link in desktop Chromium and selected “单机练习” (solo practice). The rendered hexagonal ice arena, sled riders, snowballs, eight-player survival counter, timer and ranking HUD appeared without a login, payment, installation or local setup. The simulation progressed, bots knocked players into the sea, red edge tiles marked collapsing ice, and a defeat/rank screen appeared.

This is a short desktop launch check, not a full controls or multiplayer test. Mobile touch support and shared-room cross-device play are reported by the creator and were not independently tested. The console reported a missing `favicon.ico` (HTTP 404); it did not prevent the practice match from running.

## Model attribution and implementation

In #143 the creator states that GPT-6 Astra helped implement and iterate the 3D browser prototype, evolve snowball consumption into an ice-floe elimination game, and adjust snowball growth, launch knockback and ice-collapse timing. The same submission identifies Three.js, native JavaScript and Web Audio as the stack. These are creator statements; model sessions and source code were not supplied or independently audited.
