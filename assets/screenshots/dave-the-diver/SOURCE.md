# 潜水员戴夫 / Dave the Diver — recreation

Submission: [Issue #41](https://github.com/MartinDelophy/awesome-gpt-6-astra/issues/41) by [8824PPZ](https://github.com/8824PPZ), crediting the creator as **dudu**. No separate creator profile was supplied.

Demo: https://dave-2cm.pages.dev/

`gameplay.jpg` is an unmodified 1280 × 720 browser screenshot captured from the live demo on **2026-09-08**, after starting a dive. It shows a diver among fish, oxygen at 91/100, depth at 16.6 m, the current quest and the catch inventory.

The demo opened and started a dive without login or payment. The Chinese interface provides mouse/keyboard controls and separate diving, sushi-restaurant and island-farming screens. Those screens were inspected; a complete playthrough was not performed.

The submission calls the game a Dave the Diver recreation and attributes the full development to GPT-6 Astra. The model contribution is submitter-reported, not independently audited. No public source repository or separate development record was supplied.
