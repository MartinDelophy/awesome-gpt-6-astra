## Review on 2026-09-15

The embedded browser loaded the game and displayed its controls and first-level menu without a login or installation prompt. Starting/resuming returned to the paused menu with a mouse-capture compatibility warning. A desktop Chrome attempt stalled at 28% while preparing architecture and rigged models. Gameplay movement and interaction therefore remain unverified; this does not establish that the game is broken in supported browsers.

The descriptions of the game and Astra workflow are attributed to the submitter/menu. The reported 40–50 hours of Astra-assisted development, complete three-level playthrough, final encounter and mobile compatibility have not been independently verified.

The maintainer accepted inclusion despite the review-environment limitations above. This entry does not claim a successful reviewer playthrough.
