# MoxRiding

- Creator: Moxazza / Moxazza Games, credited in the game's interface.
- Direct game: https://www.moxride.com/home/moxriding
- Submission: https://github.com/MartinDelophy/awesome-gpt-6-astra/issues/129

## Review — 2026-10-20

Opened the submitted HTTPS page in a desktop Chromium browser and started a new session in the embedded game. The city scene, session countdown, speed, trick-combo display and keyboard instructions rendered without login, installation or local setup. Keyboard movement and an ollie produced an active combo and a prompt to keep rolling before banking it; the captured session then showed 70 banked points. This is a start-and-controls check, not a full playthrough.

The in-game help describes a 90-second session with extra time earned by varied clean tricks, aerial trick buttons, magnetic rail grinds and manuals. Releasing the manual and rolling straight for two uninterrupted seconds banks the combo. The submission advertises a global top-10 leaderboard; leaderboard submission and ranking were not independently tested. Mobile compatibility was not tested, despite the submission's broad browser-support claim.

The outer Google Sites page logged CORS errors for its Google Play telemetry endpoint during the check; the embedded game still started and rendered. This does not establish that every network-dependent feature works.

## Distinct from MoxRide

The existing [MoxRide](https://www.moxride.com/) entry opens **MoxRide: Sunset Run**, a downhill race with four rivals, a route to the beach and trick-powered boost. The homepage separately links to **MoxRiding**, which presents a freely steered city district and timed trick sessions. Both live interfaces were inspected; they are listed separately rather than replacing or duplicating the old entry.

## Model attribution remains unconfirmed

Submission #129 describes repeated graphical refinement and a Street Sesh-inspired direction, but explicitly names **ChatGPT 6.1** rather than GPT-6 Astra. It supplies no model-specific conversation or development record identifying Astra. The additional image is promotional artwork, not a model-use record. The catalog therefore marks Astra participation as unconfirmed and does not equate the two model names or claim a one-prompt build.

The earlier [submission #114](https://github.com/MartinDelophy/awesome-gpt-6-astra/issues/114) concerns MoxRide: Sunset Run. Its attribution is not evidence of the model used for MoxRiding. No public game source repository was supplied in #129.
